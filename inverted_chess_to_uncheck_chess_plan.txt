1.1 SAGE
    Preserve SAGE's weight files, architecture, encoders, normal-chess
    training pipeline, model identity, and current move-ranking behavior.
    SAGE remains the model trained to win ordinary chess.
    When asked to WIN, choose its highest-ranked eligible move.
    When asked to LOSE, choose its lowest-ranked eligible move.

    First import the current GPU JESTER into both frontends. Freeze that

    JESTER remains the model trained to lose in the existing product language.
    When asked to LOSE, choose its highest-ranked eligible move.
    When asked to WIN, choose its lowest-ranked eligible move.
    Do not reset or alter any layer of these shipped weights during migration.

1.3 Exact setup matrix

    Selected game     Model    Existing goal state       Selection
    NORMAL CHESS      SAGE     asked WIN / as trained    highest eligible
    UNCHECK CHESS     SAGE     asked LOSE / opposite      lowest eligible
    UNCHECK CHESS     JESTER   asked LOSE / as trained    highest eligible
    NORMAL CHESS      JESTER   asked WIN / opposite       lowest eligible

    The rules belong to the selected GAME, and apply equally to both colors.
    Choosing a model does not give the human different legal moves.
    Existing trainedGoal and goalInverted fields may remain for compatibility:
      SAGE + goalInverted=false  -> normal
      SAGE + goalInverted=true   -> uncheck-v1

      JESTER + goalInverted=true  -> normal

1.4 Define "likely" exactly as the apps currently do
    LOW: rank by the raw policy head after determining eligible moves.
    MEDIUM/HIGH: rank by the current MCTS visit distribution, using the
    existing simulation counts, tie-breaking, and promotion handling.
    Opposite-goal selection reverses the final ranking. Do not replace it
    with an objective-aware best-move picker, a random move, or value argmin.
    Preserve current model-based search direction: SAGE non-inverted,
    JESTER inverted for both turns. Do not also reverse search because the
    user selected the opposite goal; that would change existing behavior.
    Preserve current repetition-based candidate exclusions and their
    existing exceptions. They are bot preferences, not additional game rules.
    "Eligible" means legal under the selected game and selectable under
    those existing filters. No filter may permit a non-capture when a legal
    capture exists, nor leave a moving player without a choice.

1.5 Weight equality means matching source models, not identical file bytes
    SAGE and JESTER remain DIFFERENT networks. Each model's web and mobile
    assets must represent the same respective exported model parameters.
    Web uses fp16-serialized JSON; mobile uses ONNX built from that JSON.
    Different file formats have different hashes. Record the shared source
    checkpoint hash and verify numerical inference agreement.
    Continuing GPU training later must not automatically replace these
    frontend assets. Publishing newly trained Uncheck weights is a later step.


2. EXACT UNCHECK CHESS V1 RULES

These are the proposed complete rules for implementation. The previously
unspecified corner cases are resolved here: kings cannot be captured;
castling ignores attacked-square restrictions; no-move positions are draws;
ordinary insufficient-material rules are disabled. These choices should be
used consistently across all four engines.

2.1 Board, pieces, and turns
    Use the ordinary 8x8 board, initial position, and 16 pieces per player.
    White moves first. Turns alternate. Each turn consists of one move.
    No passing, capturing friendly pieces, or multiple captures in one move.
    Piece movement, pawn double steps, sliding-piece obstruction, en passant,
    and promotion are standard except for the explicit changes below.

2.2 Win condition: resolve at the BEGINNING of the turn
    Before offering moves, inspect the king of the player whose turn begins.
    If at least one opposing piece attacks that king's square, that player
    immediately wins. No checkmate test and no escape-move test is needed.
    This victory takes precedence over draw rules and move availability.

    Consequences:
    - You may end your own turn with your own king attacked. The opponent
      gets one move to remove every attack by their pieces on your king.
    - If your king is still attacked when your turn returns, you win.
    - If you end your move attacking the opponent's king, the opponent wins
      immediately as their turn begins. They do NOT get a rescue turn.
    - If both kings are attacked after a move, the next player wins first.
    - The attacker need not be the same piece on the two successive turns.
      The only test is whether any attack exists at the turn boundary.
    - An imported position with the side-to-move king attacked is already
      terminal. Do not generate a move or evaluate the network for that side.

    This corrects the earlier wording about giving an opponent check. The
    one-turn rescue applies to a king exposed during ITS OWNER'S move.

2.3 What "attacked" means
    Use ordinary attack geometry, independently of legal move generation:
    pawn diagonals, knight jumps, adjacent king squares, and unobstructed
    bishop/rook/queen rays. A pawn's forward push is not an attack.
    Include attacks by pieces that would be pinned under ordinary chess.
    A compulsory capture elsewhere does not switch off an existing attack.
    The prohibition on capturing kings does not switch off attacks on kings.
    Do not compute attacks by asking which legal king captures are available.
    A piece only attacks through a blocker if that blocker actually moves.

2.4 King movement and capture
    Kings may move into attacked squares or expose themselves by moving
    another piece. Ordinary self-check restrictions do not apply.
    Kings are never captured and never removed from the board.
    Kings may capture opposing non-king pieces using normal king movement.
    Such king captures count for the compulsory-capture rule.
    A move onto the enemy king's occupied square is always forbidden.
    Moving adjacent to the enemy king is allowed geometrically, but attacks
    both kings and gives the next player the immediate victory.

2.5 Compulsory captures
    Generate every movement-valid move, including moves exposing your king,
    while excluding king captures. Then identify all capturing moves.
    If any capture exists anywhere on the board, ALL non-capturing moves
    are unavailable. The player may choose any available capture.
    There is no requirement to maximize captured value or prefer king moves.
    En passant and capturing promotions are captures.
    Quiet promotions and castling are non-captures.
    Capture detection must inspect the actual captured pawn for en passant,
    because its destination square is empty.
    Capture priority applies even when a quiet move could rescue the enemy
    king. A legal capture that fails to rescue that king remains legal and
    results in the opponent winning at the next turn boundary.

2.6 Unchecking the opponent
    To prevent their victory, finish your move with NONE of your pieces
    attacking their king. You can withdraw an attacker or interpose a piece.
    A capturing move can also move an attacker away or block a different ray.
    You cannot move the opponent's king or capture your own attacking piece.
    Removing one attack while uncovering another does not rescue the king.
    There is no separate obligation that forbids losing moves. Always apply
    the normal Uncheck move list and compulsory-capture filter, then the win
    test after changing the side to move.

2.7 Castling
    Keep the normal castling starting/destination squares, unmoved king and
    rook requirements, rook presence, and empty intervening squares.
    Ignore attack restrictions on the origin, transit, and destination.
    In practice, a player whose king is attacked at turn start already won,
    so castling out of check is never played in a nonterminal game.
    Castling is unavailable whenever that player has a permitted capture.
    Apply both piece movements before checking the next player's king.

2.8 Promotions and en passant
    Promotion must choose queen, rook, bishop, or knight; never king or pawn.
    All four options remain distinct legal moves and search branches.
    Resolve the promotion before evaluating attacks and the result.
    En passant lasts for the immediately following turn as in normal chess.
    Remove the captured pawn before evaluating discovered attacks.

2.9 Draws and termination order
    After a move: update board, rights, en passant, clocks, and history;
    change side to move; then apply this exact priority:
      1. Side-to-move king attacked -> that player wins.
      2. Third occurrence of the same position -> draw.
      3. 100 half-moves without a pawn move or capture -> draw.
      4. No permitted move -> draw.
      5. Otherwise continue.
    Seed the initial position's occurrence count at one.
    Repetition identity includes pieces, side to move, castling rights,
    effective en-passant availability, and ruleset. An en-passant square
    matters only when it supplies a permitted capture. Exclude move clocks.
    Do not introduce this repetition-key change into NORMAL CHESS silently;
    preserve its current behavior in its own branch.
    No automatic draw for bare kings, king+bishop, king+knight, or any other
    ordinary insufficient-mating-material classification in Uncheck v1.
    These positions can still terminate under the Uncheck attack rule.
    Existing agreed draws and resignations may remain available: agreement
    draws, and a resignation awards the other player the actual victory.
    A training-only move cap is a truncation, not a proved draw or a new
    gameplay rule. Timed chess is outside the scope of this migration.

2.10 Canonical tactical example
    FEN: r6k/8/5n2/8/8/3K4/P7/8 w - - 0 1
    White: king d3, pawn a2. Black: king h8, knight f6, rook a8.
    White plays d3e4, entering the knight's attack.
    Black's only permitted capture is a8a2. Capturing White's king is banned.
    Black must capture a2 and cannot instead move the knight away.
    White's turn begins with its king still attacked: White wins.

Import receipt and validation results are appended at the end of this file.

Procedure used / required for reproducible future imports:
  1. Record both repositories' dirty state and hashes of all SAGE assets.
  2. Back up the existing JESTER JSON, ONNX, and metadata before replacement.
  3. Work in isolated Git worktrees. Preserve unrelated local changes.
  4. Connect with ssh gpus. If MagicDNS fails, try ssh gpus-ts. These aliases

     A hard link to the completed latest.pt therefore pins one stable inode

  6. Hash the remote snapshot, copy it with scp, and verify the local hash.

         --output assets/models/jester.onnx \
         --training-generation CHECKPOINT_GENERATION \
         --checkpoint-sha256 CHECKPOINT_SHA256

     The local exporter already supports the last two provenance arguments.
     Preserve its existing local changes when assembling a future release.
     Never rely on the exporter's default output: that default is sage.onnx.
  9. Update JESTER metadata and its pinned model-provenance test values.
     Preserve SAGE's metadata, tensors, hash pins, and test assertions.
 10. Check actual frontend inference, JSON/ONNX numerical parity, architecture
     compatibility, provenance hashes, build, and targeted existing tests.

    The optimized expansion path must agree with get_moves + apply_move.
    Expose a ruleset/engine-protocol capability marker; refuse an old binary
    when Uncheck is requested. Rebuild the extension before GPU training.

5.3 Search-value convention: preserve existing model-based direction
    Keep the public game result and internal reference value separate.
    Let z_actual(s, player) be +1 when that player wins the selected game,
    -1 when they lose, and 0 for a rule-defined draw.

      normal reference target:       z_ref =  z_actual
      uncheck-v1 reference target:   z_ref = -z_actual

    This preserves the existing inverted-selection architecture for JESTER.
    For Uncheck, the internal WDL channels mean reference-win/reference-draw/
    reference-loss; reference-loss corresponds to ACTUAL Uncheck victory.
    Do not call these ordinary-chess winning probabilities in Uncheck mode.

    Concrete invariant: at an Uncheck terminal node, the player to move is
    the actual winner, so their reference terminal value is -1. A JESTER
    inverted search favors that outcome for itself, just as the current
    search favors self-mate under the legacy reference convention.

    A White Uncheck win labels White positions -1 and Black positions +1
    in reference-value training; the dashboard must still report White WIN.
    A Black Uncheck win reverses these labels. Rule-defined draws use 0.
    Keep sign alternation during backup and the current model-based PUCT
    selection directions. Do not also negate the evaluator, swap WDL outputs,
    or turn off JESTER inversion: each would introduce a double inversion.

    Frozen SAGE and old JESTER nonterminal estimates will be imperfect under
    the new game. That limitation is accepted for this weights-preserving
    migration. Only terminal/search rule handling and future training targets
    adopt the new reference semantics. Do not edit the saved neural tensors.

5.4 training/src/chess_ai/mcts.py and training/rust_engine/src/mcts.rs
    Update every terminal check, root fast path, leaf expansion, batched
    search, draw shortcut, repetition path, and worker adapter for Uncheck.
    Check Uncheck victory before material, repetition, or cap shortcuts.
    Disable ordinary insufficient-material checks in Uncheck searches.
    Preserve all promotion branches even though their 4096-policy slot is
    shared. Aggregate visits only at the neural target boundary, not in
    child-state identity. Keep the current architecture and action size.
    Retain current search-only terminal-distance preferences consistently;
    do not decay training outcome labels or turn distant wins into draws.

5.5 training/src/chess_ai/encoding.py and weight_io.py
    Keep the existing 20 input planes, board orientation, 4096 outputs, tensor

    Uncheck's turn-boundary win is derivable from board plus side to move;

    Keep repetition history in the engine/search as already needed.
    Record ruleset/value convention in metadata rather than changing shapes.
